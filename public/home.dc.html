About Us

Sunday Afternoon Bonkhouse grew out of five years of art nerd sickos coming together to watch the very BEST and very WORST of horror cult classics on the big screen. Each October, we assemble a themed double feature and cut retro era trailers, commercials, shorts, and animations from people we love into custom pre-shows that turn the double feature into its own strange little event.
